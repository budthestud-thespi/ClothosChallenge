Dropquote

The letters above each column drop into the squares directly below them to spell a short verse. Black squares separate words. Tap a square and type; letters cross off their column as you use them.

How to solve Start with short words and squares whose column has only one letter left, then use the crossed-off letters to whittle down what's left in each column.

As you finish the phrase, Clotho’s eyes turn milky white. She says nothing else, yet points to the words floating in the air behind you.
